Teams Squared passed its Stage 2 ISO 27001 audit on 31 August 2026 and received certification. I led much of the implementation and preparation as ISMS Officer.

## What the auditor checked

The auditor checked whether our Information Security Management System (ISMS) operated as documented. That included following individual controls from the Statement of Applicability to the process in use and the records showing it had run.

Before the audit, I practiced explaining those connections in both directions: from a risk to its control and evidence, and from a setting in the tenant back to the reason for it. This helped me find gaps while there was still time to address them.

Identity controls were among the easier areas to demonstrate. Entra ID and Microsoft 365 were already in use, and we had records from access reviews, joiner-mover-leaver processes, and conditional access. Areas where I had documented the work later were harder to prepare.

## What happens after certification

Certification confirms that an external body assessed the management system and found it conformant at that time. It does not guarantee security or remove the need to keep reviewing the system. Next year's surveillance audit will look at the work we do between audits.

We still have processes that rely on someone remembering to perform a task or keep a record. I want to make those easier to run consistently, using the same approach as the controls that already produce records through routine work.

## The team's role

Although much of the implementation sat with me, this took support from other people. Having the ISMS Owner involved at executive level helped us get time and attention for the operational changes.

Now we need to keep up the reviews, evidence collection, and corrective actions that the system requires.
