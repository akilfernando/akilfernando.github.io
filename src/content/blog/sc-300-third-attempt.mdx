I passed Microsoft's SC-300 Identity and Access Administrator Associate exam on my third attempt. After the first two attempts, I changed how I studied and how I worked through the questions.

## Using the feedback

The score report gave me broad topic areas to revisit rather than per-question feedback. I used the weaker domains to decide what to study again instead of trying to guess which individual questions I had missed.

## Practicing scenarios

Reading the material until it felt familiar was not enough for me. I started practicing scenario questions and explaining why I would choose one control over another. I also worked through why the remaining options did not fit the situation.

## Working in Entra ID

My day job gave me practical experience with MFA, app registrations, and identity governance. Configuring those features and seeing how they behaved helped me understand material I had previously only read about.

## Reading more carefully

I slowed down on questions with multiple answers and checked each option individually. If I could not explain why an option was right or wrong, I treated that as a topic I needed to revisit.

For another attempt, I would use the domain feedback to plan revision, practice decisions in context, and spend time using the features in a tenant where possible.
