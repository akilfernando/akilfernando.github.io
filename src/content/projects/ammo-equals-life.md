Ammo = Life is a first-person shooter I built in Unity. Your ammunition is also your health, so firing a shot reduces both. Picking up ammunition is the only way to recover.

I wanted to see how using one resource for both would affect the game. It made accuracy more important, and ammo placement became a way to control how much risk the player could take. Players had to decide whether firing at an enemy was worth losing some of their health.

The demo is rough and was never intended as a full release. I used it in my portfolio when applying for the gameplay programming co-op at Ubisoft Halifax, and it helped me get the role.
