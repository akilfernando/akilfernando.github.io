I inherited an unreleased client portal at Teams Squared and became its sole developer. The original app showed clients information pulled from other services. I expanded it to handle client management and contractor workflows, replacing Zelt and Clockify.

Clients can review team engagements, invoices, and approvals. Contractors can log hours, view timesheets, and request leave. I integrated Resend for transactional emails and configured Microsoft Entra ID authentication.

The application uses a React and TypeScript front end with a Python and FastAPI back end. I used Claude Code and Google Antigravity during development, alongside staging and production environments and automated deployment pipelines.

I wrote about the rollout and the changes I would make to the user experience in [Inheriting a production app](/blog/inheriting-a-production-app/).
