Happbit is a small, cross-platform habit tracker I built for a Mobile Development course at Dalhousie University.

You add a habit and check it off when you complete it. I kept the app focused on that workflow, without a social feed, streaks, gamification, or notifications. The project was a chance to practice building a simple interface and deciding which features it actually needed.
